Privacy

This portfolio site does not use advertising cookies.

Analytics

This site uses basic analytics to understand traffic (such as page views and referrers) and improve the site. No advertising cookies are used. I do not sell your personal information.

Inquiries

If you submit an inquiry, the information you enter (such as your name, email, organization, and message) is sent to me and may be stored in a Google Spreadsheet so I can respond and track requests. Please avoid sharing sensitive information in the inquiry form.

Data retention

Inquiry submissions are kept only as long as needed to respond and manage client relationships. I periodically review and delete older inquiry records (typically within 12–24 months) unless a longer retention period is required for legitimate business purposes.

Access controls

Access to inquiry records is restricted to authorized accounts only. I review access permissions to the inquiry spreadsheet on a recurring basis (at least quarterly) to ensure only the minimum necessary access is granted.

Deletion requests

If you would like your inquiry information removed, contact me and I will take reasonable steps to delete it from my records, subject to any legal or operational requirements.
